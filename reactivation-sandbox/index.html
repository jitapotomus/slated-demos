Reactivation sandbox

This is the "Book a time" page the campaign emails link to. Use it to test the whole loop.

You came here from a campaign email. Send the form below and the campaign's "Booked" count goes up.

  1. Open the Past customers tab on the demo owner page (Desktop shortcut "Reactivation sandbox").
  2. Press Load sample customers, then pick a campaign and press Send campaign.
  3. Check your inbox: emails arrive marked [DEMO] (they would go to the customers on a real site).
  4. In an email, tap the Book a time link. It opens this page. Send the form.
  5. Back on the Past customers tab, the campaign shows Booked: 1. Try the call list buttons and the unsubscribe link too.
