Questions

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We take a look and give you a written estimate. There's no cost and no pressure to say yes.

Can I fix a broken spring myself?

We don't recommend it. Springs hold a lot of tension and can cause serious injury. Call us instead.

Should I repair or replace my door?

Many problems are a simple repair. We'll tell you honestly when a new door makes more sense.

Why does my door go back up when it closes?

Often it's the safety sensors being blocked or out of line. We can check and fix it.

Do you serve my town?

We're based in Boise and serve nearby towns like Meridian, Nampa, Eagle and Kuna. Not sure? Just call and ask.
