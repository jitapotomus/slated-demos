Privacy

CFB Construction, Greenback, TN. Last updated November 27, 2026.

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you send us through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into our request form: your name, phone number, email (if you give one), address, the day and time you prefer, and your message. If you use a quote tool, we also keep the jobs you picked and the estimate you saw.

Why we collect it

To answer your request, give you a quote, and book a time. We do not use it for anything else.

Who sees it

  • CFB Construction, who reads your request and contacts you.
  • The booking service that runs this form (Slated Studio) and the email and hosting services it uses to deliver and store your request. They handle it only to run this website.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

AI helpers on this site

If you use the quote assistant or phone assistant, you are talking to an AI. It helps build an estimate or take a message. What you type into the assistant is sent to AI service providers so they can write the reply. It does not set final prices: the business reviews every request. Phone assistants tell you when a call is recorded. Please do not type card numbers, bank details or other sensitive information into any form or chat.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to serve you and to keep a record of the work. You can ask us to delete your request at any time. If this business stops using the booking service, the stored requests are deleted within 30 days.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or trackers.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we have about you. Please call (865) 221-4955. We will answer within a few business days.

Children

This website is for adults who want to hire us. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes too.
