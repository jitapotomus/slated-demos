Questions

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We take a look and give you a written estimate. There's no cost and no pressure to say yes.

Do you charge to come out?

Call us and ask. We'll explain how pricing works before anyone comes to your home.

Can you fix my water heater or do I need a new one?

It depends on its age and what's wrong. We'll check it and give you honest numbers for both.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off the main water valve, then call us. Shutting the water off first saves a lot of damage.
