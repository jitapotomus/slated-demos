Let's take a look at your roof

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We take a look and give you a written estimate. There's no cost and no pressure to say yes.

How do I know if I need a repair or a new roof?

It depends on the roof's age and how widespread the damage is. We'll show you what we found and tell you honestly if a repair will do the job.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. We check for wind and hail damage, take photos, and explain your options for repair or replacement.

How long does a new roof take?

Many homes are done in a day or two, depending on size, weather and materials. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.

Do you serve my town?

We're based in Omaha and serve nearby towns like Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista and Elkhorn. Not sure? Just call and ask.
